How 2 Player Bohnanza Works

Bohnanza is a bean planting and trading card game. 
A key rule to this game is:
You can’t change the order of the cards in your hand.
You can only plant the same bean in a bean field
This rule is what makes all your decisions matter.

Setup

Each player gets  5 bean cards (you cant rearrange them).

Each player has two bean fields to start.

The goal is to earn coins by planting and harvesting beans.

Your Turn (2 Player Rules)
1. Plant Beans

You MUST plant the first card in your hand.
You MAY plant the second card if you want.
If the bean type doesn’t match anything in your fields, you must harvest a field to make space.

2. Flip Over 3 Cards

In 2 player mode, the computer will reveal three cards  from the deck into the offer area.

These cards can be planted or discarded or you can leave them alone  

3. Draw New Cards

At the end, you draw two cards, adding them to the back of your hand in the order drawn.

4. Next player will either plant or discard the cards left from previouse player in step 2 and then repeat the steps

End of the Game

The game is played untill the deck is finished .
After the deck is finished, both players count their coins.
The player with the most coins wins.
If the amount of coins are doubled then the player with the most cards
in their hand wins!
